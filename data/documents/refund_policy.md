# Cloudify Refund Policy

## Overview

This policy explains how Cloudify handles refunds for subscription charges, plan changes, and service cancellations. Cloudify is a SaaS company offering infrastructure and developer operations services to customers across the Starter, Professional, and Enterprise plans. This policy should be read together with the [subscription_policy.md](subscription_policy.md), [billing_guide.md](billing_guide.md), and [account_management.md](account_management.md) documents.

## 1. Standard refund window

Cloudify offers a 14-day refund window for all new subscriptions, including monthly and annual products, starting on the date the customer first receives access to the service. Customers may request a refund within that period for the unused portion of the current billing cycle.

### Eligibility

Customers are eligible for a refund only if all of the following are true:

- The request is submitted within 14 calendar days of the initial purchase date or the first activation date for a new account.
- The account is not in material breach of the [acceptable_use_policy.md](acceptable_use_policy.md), [security_policy.md](security_policy.md), or [subscription_policy.md](subscription_policy.md).
- The customer has not materially misrepresented their usage or payment status.
- The service has not been subject to a prior chargeback, fraud review, or payment dispute.

### Non-refundable situations

Refunds are not available in the following cases:

- More than 14 days after the initial purchase or first activation.
- Renewal charges for an annual plan after the 14-day cooling-off window.
- Charges for usage beyond plan allocations, overages, premium support add-ons, or professional services already delivered.
- Payment disputes resolved by a bank or card issuer without Cloudify review.
- Accounts terminated due to accepted policy violations or fraud.

## 2. Annual subscriptions

Annual subscriptions are invoiced in advance. Cloudify permits a full refund during the 14-day cooling-off period only. After that period, annual subscriptions are generally non-refundable unless Cloudify terminates the service for a material issue under the [sla.md](sla.md) or [support_policy.md](support_policy.md).

### Annual cancellation rules

If a customer cancels an annual subscription after the 14-day window:

- The service remains active through the end of the current billing term.
- No refund is issued for the unused portion of the annual commitment after the cooling-off period.
- Any prorated credits for unused time are only considered if the customer is experiencing an agreed service failure under the SLA or if Cloudify initiates termination for cause.

## 3. Plan-specific treatment

### Starter

- Monthly Starter subscriptions may be refunded within 14 days of activation.
- Annual Starter subscriptions follow the same 14-day refund rule; after day 14, they are non-refundable.

### Professional

- Monthly Professional subscriptions may be refunded within 14 days of activation.
- Annual Professional subscriptions follow the same 14-day rule and are otherwise non-refundable after the cooling-off period.

### Enterprise

- Enterprise contracts are annual and negotiated individually.
- Enterprise customers may request a refund within 14 days of the contract start date, subject to any setup fees already incurred or approved professional services already rendered.
- After the first 14 days, enterprises are generally bound to the annual commitment unless the contract contains a termination for convenience clause or a material service failure under the [sla.md](sla.md).

## 4. Subscription changes and proration

Customers may upgrade or downgrade their plan at any time. For monthly billing, the new plan begins immediately, and the charge is prorated for the remainder of the billing cycle. For annual plans, plan changes take effect on the next renewal date unless otherwise stated in the contract.

For plan downgrades:

- The credit for the remaining unused time is applied as a credit toward the next invoice, not as a cash refund.
- Credits do not carry over beyond the current billing cycle.

For upgrades:

- Additional charges are prorated from the date of the change.

## 5. Premium add-ons and professional services

Cloudify does not provide refunds for any of the following:

- Professional services already delivered.
- Dedicated onboarding or migration work.
- Data migration assistance.
- Premium support packages when the service period has begun.
- Overage fees, usage spikes, or API overage charges already incurred.

## 6. Refund request procedure

Customers must submit a refund request through the Cloudify billing portal or by contacting Cloudify support. Requests should include:

- Account name and organization ID.
- Plan and billing period.
- Date of purchase or activation.
- Invoice number.
- Reason for the request.
- Any supporting information relevant to service issues.

Cloudify reviews refund requests within 5 business days. The review may involve account, usage, and billing checks under [billing_guide.md](billing_guide.md) and [support_policy.md](support_policy.md).

## 7. Chargebacks and payment disputes

Customers should contact Cloudify billing support before filing a chargeback or payment dispute. If a chargeback is filed without prior notice, Cloudify may suspend the account pending review. Cloudify may restrict access to support, APIs, and admin capabilities during the investigation under [account_management.md](account_management.md).

## 8. Service credits and compensation

The refund policy does not replace SLA service credits described in [sla.md](sla.md). If a customer is eligible for service credits under the SLA, that credit is applied according to the SLA schedule and may be combined with a refund only where explicitly stated in a negotiated Enterprise agreement.

## 9. Policy updates

Cloudify may revise this policy from time to time. Any material change takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle for existing customers, except where required by law or contract. Customers are responsible for reviewing changes to plan terms and policies as reflected in the customer portal and account documentation.
