# Cloudify Enterprise Plan

## Overview

The Cloudify Enterprise plan is designed for organizations that require enhanced uptime, broader governance, stronger security, and faster production support. This plan builds on the Professional plan and includes custom commitments that may be defined in the customer’s signed contract.

This document should be read with [sla.md](sla.md), [support_policy.md](support_policy.md), [security_policy.md](security_policy.md), [subscription_policy.md](subscription_policy.md), and [account_management.md](account_management.md).

## 1. Enterprise scope

Enterprise is intended for organizations with production-critical workloads, multiple teams, or formal governance requirements. Standard enterprise features include:

- 99.95% monthly uptime commitment.
- 24x7 support coverage for all severities.
- Priority handling for incidents and escalations.
- Dedicated technical and billing contacts.
- Advanced security controls, including SSO, enhanced audit logging, and security review support.
- Custom environment counts and project limits defined in the signed agreement.

## 2. Support and service commitments

Enterprise customers receive:

- 15-minute acknowledgement for Severity 1 issues.
- 1-hour acknowledgement for Severity 2 issues.
- 4-business-hour acknowledgement for Severity 3 issues.
- 1-business-day acknowledgement for Severity 4 issues.

These support commitments are in addition to the SLA protections described in [sla.md](sla.md), and they may be superseded by a negotiated agreement if more specific wording is provided in the contract.

## 3. Security and governance

Enterprise customers receive additional controls beyond the base plan, including:

- SSO and SCIM integration options.
- Multi-region or private-network compatibility where available.
- Enhanced access review and governance workflows.
- Extended retention and audit support.
- Security review participation for production environment rollout and incident review.

The security responsibilities and access boundaries for Enterprise customers are described in [security_policy.md](security_policy.md) and [privacy_policy.md](privacy_policy.md).

## 4. Billing and contract terms

Enterprise plans are typically annual and contract-based. Billing is negotiated with Cloudify before onboarding and is set out in the customer agreement. Standard enterprise billing terms include annual invoicing, custom pricing, and customer-specific limits or support add-ons.

Refunds for Enterprise subscriptions follow [refund_policy.md](refund_policy.md). Enterprise contracts may include different cancellation terms from the standard 14-day refund window, but any such terms must be explicitly stated in the signed agreement.

## 5. Eligibility and onboarding

Enterprise onboarding begins only after:

- A commercial agreement is signed.
- Security and compliance review is completed where required.
- Billing and technical contacts are confirmed.
- Support escalation paths are established.

Enterprise onboarding and account processes are described in [onboarding_guide.md](onboarding_guide.md) and [account_management.md](account_management.md).

## 6. Service credits and compensation

Enterprise customers are eligible for the service credit schedule defined in [sla.md](sla.md). Where an outage or production incident materially affects the customer’s business operations, Cloudify may also consider an additional credit or contract-specific remedy if the signed agreement allows it.

## 7. Limits and responsibilities

Enterprise customers are expected to:

- Maintain secure identity controls for their organization.
- Provide accurate owner and contact information.
- Ensure that internal usage and operational requirements are clearly communicated.
- Comply with all policy obligations described in [acceptable_use_policy.md](acceptable_use_policy.md) and [api_usage_policy.md](api_usage_policy.md).
