# Cloudify Privacy Policy

## Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Cloudify collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data for customers, users, and administrators of Cloudify products, including the Cloudify control plane, deployment telemetry, support interactions, and billing records. Cloudify supports the Starter, Professional, and Enterprise subscription plans.

This policy should be read together with the [security_policy.md](security_policy.md), [data_retention.md](data_retention.md), [account_management.md](account_management.md), and [support_policy.md](support_policy.md).

## 1. Data we collect

Cloudify processes the following categories of information:

### Account and contact data

- Full name and contact details for account owners and administrators.
- Business name, billing address, VAT or tax identifiers where required.
- Login identifiers, user roles, and MFA enrollment status.
- Payment information processed by Cloudify’s payment provider, not stored in the primary Cloudify environment.

### Service usage data

- Deployment metadata, project names, environment types, and infrastructure topology.
- API usage logs, request totals, rate-limit counters, and error events.
- Workspace activity history, configuration changes, and audit events.
- Operational telemetry: CPU, memory, storage, network, and health-check data.

### Support and communication data

- Ticket content, chat transcripts, attachments, and escalation notes.
- Customer satisfaction ratings and issue classification metadata.
- Internal notes created by Cloudify support and engineering teams.

### Security and compliance data

- MFA and SSO login records.
- Security event logs and device metadata used for access control and investigation.
- Certificate and key metadata relevant to secure service access.

## 2. Purposes of processing

Cloudify uses personal and operational data to:

- Deliver the Cloudify platform and manage customer subscriptions.
- Validate account ownership and maintain administrative controls.
- Process billing, invoicing, and payment collection.
- Provide support, incident response, and operational monitoring.
- Maintain uptime, compliance posture, and security investigations.
- Develop and improve the quality of Cloudify services and customer experience.
- Comply with legal obligations, tax requirements, and contractual commitments.

## 3. Lawful basis and customer responsibilities

Cloudify processes personal data on the basis of contract performance, legitimate business interests, and legal compliance. Customers are responsible for ensuring they have lawful permission to provide personal data to Cloudify for valid business use. Customer administrators maintain responsibility for the data they process in Cloudify environments and for ensuring that user permissions and data handling policies align with their internal governance requirements.

## 4. Access controls and data minimization

Cloudify restricts access to customer data to personnel who require it for their role, including support, engineering, and billing operations. Access is determined by role-based permissions and reviewed quarterly for compliance with least-privilege principles. Additional access restrictions apply to Enterprise contracts under the [enterprise_plan.md](enterprise_plan.md) and [security_policy.md](security_policy.md).

Cloudify minimizes the collection and retention of personal data to that which is necessary for the service, in line with [data_retention.md](data_retention.md).

## 5. Sharing with third parties

Cloudify may share data with subprocessors that provide:

- Payment processing.
- Hosting and infrastructure operations.
- Monitoring and observability.
- Support tooling and ticketing.
- Security monitoring and compliance review.

Cloudify requires subcontractors to maintain contractual commitments for confidentiality, security, and lawful processing.

## 6. International transfer

Cloudify may transfer customer data across jurisdictions in connection with hosting, support, and operations. Where transfer to a region outside the customer’s primary operating jurisdiction occurs, Cloudify uses contractual safeguards and region-specific controls consistent with applicable privacy laws.

## 7. Customer rights

Depending on jurisdiction, customers may have rights to:

- Request access to personal data held by Cloudify.
- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
- Request deletion of their data, subject to lawful retention requirements.
- Restrict or object to certain processing activities.
- Receive a copy of their data in a portable format.
- Withdraw consent where consent is the relevant lawful basis.

Requests are processed under the procedures in [data_retention.md](data_retention.md) and [account_management.md](account_management.md). Cloudify may deny requests when legal or contractual obligations require retention, including tax, billing, security, and fraud review records.

## 8. Retention and deletion

Cloudify retains different categories of data according to the schedule described in [data_retention.md](data_retention.md). Customer data is retained only for as long as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or maintain business records.

### Standard retention windows

- Billing records: 7 years.
- Support records: 3 years for standard plans; 5 years for Enterprise customers where the contract requires long-term support and audit records.
- Telemetry and operational logs: 90 days for Starter, 180 days for Professional, and 1 year for Enterprise where retention is specified in the contract.
- Backups: retained for up to 30 days, except where stricter compliance or customer contractual terms require longer retention.

## 9. Security of personal data

Cloudify secures customer data using encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, MFA, audit logging, and incident detection procedures. Additional security controls for Enterprise customers are described in [security_policy.md](security_policy.md) and [enterprise_plan.md](enterprise_plan.md).

## 10. Contact and escalation

Customers seeking to exercise privacy rights, ask questions about processing, or report a privacy concern should contact Cloudify Privacy and Data Protection at privacy@cloudify.example or through the Cloudify support portal. Cloudify will acknowledge all privacy inquiries within 5 business days and resolve them according to applicable law and contract.
