# Cloudify Acceptable Use Policy

## Overview

This Acceptable Use Policy defines the behaviors and workloads that are permitted on the Cloudify platform. The purpose of this policy is to ensure that Cloudify remains stable, secure, and commercially viable for all customers across the Starter, Professional, and Enterprise plans.

This document is linked to [api_usage_policy.md](api_usage_policy.md), [security_policy.md](security_policy.md), [subscription_policy.md](subscription_policy.md), and [support_policy.md](support_policy.md).

## 1. Permitted use

Cloudify customers may use the platform to:

- Deploy and manage cloud infrastructure and application environments.
- Support internal engineering, DevOps, and infrastructure workflows.
- Operate workloads that are consistent with the customer’s plan type and contract.
- Use Cloudify APIs and user interfaces for authorized administrative tasks.

## 2. Prohibited use

Customers may not use Cloudify to:

- Launch malicious, abusive, or harmful infrastructure or applications.
- Operate botnets, credential stuffing tools, scanning activity, or spam infrastructure.
- Exfiltrate data, impersonate third parties, or commit fraud.
- Circumvent billing, rate limits, or identity verification safeguards.
- Host illegal or regulated workloads outside the customer’s governance and compliance obligations.
- Consume excessive platform resources beyond the plan’s approved limits or the account’s permitted boundaries.

## 3. Security and abuse response

Cloudify may suspend or restrict a customer account if it detects abuse, security compromise, or material violations of this policy. Where the issue is a suspected security event, Cloudify may temporarily suspend access pending investigation under [security_policy.md](security_policy.md) and [incident_response.md](incident_response.md).

## 4. API and resource usage

All API and infrastructure usage must comply with [api_usage_policy.md](api_usage_policy.md). Customers must not attempt to bypass limits or use the platform for mass automation or processing outside the scope of the agreed subscription. Excessive resource use may require a plan upgrade or a contract amendment.

## 5. Content and data obligations

Customers remain responsible for any data, content, or workloads they deploy to Cloudify. They must ensure that all content they process is legal and that any third-party data processed through Cloudify is appropriately authorized and secured.

## 6. Support and compliance impact

Violations of this policy may lead to:

- Warning notices.
- Rate limit reductions.
- Temporary or permanent suspension of API or account access.
- Termination under the terms in [subscription_policy.md](subscription_policy.md).
- Financial consequences if the violation results in infrastructure damage, abuse, or support burden.

## 7. Plan distinctions

### Starter

Starter customers are expected to remain within the defined usage and protection model. Resource-heavy or abusive workloads may be restricted without notice to protect service quality for other customers.

### Professional

Professional customers may operate more complex workloads but are still subject to rate limits, abuse prevention, and security safeguards.

### Enterprise

Enterprise customers have higher usage allowances and stronger support commitments, but they remain subject to the same prohibitions and security rules. Enterprise agreements may also include additional governance requirements and custom limit definitions.

## 8. Review and updates

Cloudify may revise this policy to reflect changes in technical, operational, or legal requirements. Customers are expected to review updates communicated through account notices or policy changes in their portal.
