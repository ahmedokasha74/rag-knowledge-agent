# Cloudify Documentation Library

## What Cloudify is

Cloudify is a fictional SaaS company that provides cloud infrastructure services and developer operations tooling for engineering organizations. Cloudify helps customers manage infrastructure lifecycles, automate deployment workflows, maintain secure environments, and coordinate operations across development, staging, and production workloads.

## What this documentation represents

This directory contains the internal knowledge base for the fictional Cloudify business. The documents reflect realistic SaaS governance, billing, support, security, and account policies used by the current Basic RAG pipeline.

The set includes operational knowledge about:

- Subscription plans and service entitlements.
- Billing and refund rules.
- Support and incident handling.
- Security, privacy, and retention commitments.
- Account management and onboarding.
- API usage and acceptable use boundaries.

## Subscription plans

Cloudify offers three subscription plans:

1. Starter
   - Intended for smaller teams and early-stage deployments.
   - Includes standard support and a 99.5% monthly uptime SLA.

2. Professional
   - Intended for growing engineering organizations.
   - Includes priority support and a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA.

3. Enterprise
   - Intended for production-critical organizations with enhanced governance and support needs.
   - Includes 24x7 support and a 99.95% monthly uptime SLA.

## Purpose of each document

- refund_policy.md: refund windows, annual cancellation rules, and billing exceptions.
- privacy_policy.md: personal data handling, rights, and lawful processing.
- security_policy.md: platform security requirements and customer responsibilities.
- sla.md: uptime commitments, credits, and exclusions.
- onboarding_guide.md: setup, provisioning, and initial account activation steps.
- support_policy.md: support tiers, response times, and escalation processes.
- subscription_policy.md: plan definitions, entitlements, and billing schedule rules.
- billing_guide.md: invoices, plan changes, credits, and payment behavior.
- acceptable_use_policy.md: prohibited activity and platform boundaries.
- account_management.md: user roles, ownership, account changes, and termination.
- enterprise_plan.md: enterprise-only commitments and support features.
- incident_response.md: severity classification, communications, and remediation.
- data_retention.md: retention windows, deletion, and legal hold rules.
- api_usage_policy.md: API limits, security rules, and abuse handling.

## RAG use

These documents are intentionally written as a coherent internal knowledge base for the retrieval-augmented generation system. They support questions that require combining multiple references, such as:

- What is the refund policy for an annual Enterprise plan?
- What uptime does the Enterprise plan guarantee?
- How long does Cloudify retain customer support records?
- What support response time does an Enterprise customer receive?
- What happens during a major platform outage?
- What API rate limits apply to Professional customers?

The documents are intentionally interconnected to support future GraphRAG and Hybrid RAG experiments. Structured customer, order, and support data remain outside this directory.
